    Airborne Software & OSs
    Safety & Security

    My work focuses on Formal Methods and on the Development, Verification, and Airworthiness Certification of Airborne Software, especiaaly airborne operating systems. My interests also extend to safety- and security-critical systems in space, nuclear power, and automotive applications. I contribute to the development of functional safety standards for industrial operating systems and support Common Criteria (CC) certification for operating systems and trusted execution environments.
